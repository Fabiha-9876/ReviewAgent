# Synthetic Data Validation Guide

## For Expert Reviewers

### Background

We are building ReviewAgent, a system that automatically classifies app store reviews into categories (bug report, feature request, performance complaint, usability issue, compatibility issue, praise). To train the classifier, we use a combination of real labeled data (MAALEJ dataset, 5,008 human-annotated reviews) and 500 synthetic reviews generated by a template-based script.

The synthetic reviews are needed because the MAALEJ dataset only covers 4 categories and completely lacks performance complaints and compatibility issues. The synthetic data fills those gaps.

Your task is to validate whether the synthetic reviews are realistic enough to be used as training data.

---

### What You Will Evaluate

**File:** `Synthetic_Data_Validation_Form.xlsx`

The spreadsheet has 4 sheets:

**Sheet 1 — Instructions:** Read this first.

**Sheet 2 — Review Samples (150 reviews):** 25 randomly selected reviews from each of the 6 categories. For each review, you will score:
- **Realism (1-5):** Does this sound like a real app store review?
  - 1 = Completely artificial
  - 2 = Mostly artificial
  - 3 = Acceptable, could pass as real
  - 4 = Realistic
  - 5 = Indistinguishable from real
- **Category Correctness (Y/N):** Is the assigned category right?
- **Comments:** Any observations, suggestions, flagged issues.

**Sheet 3 — Process Validation:** Shows the templates and word lists used to generate reviews. You will evaluate whether:
- The templates capture real review patterns
- The word lists are current and comprehensive
- The category distribution is realistic
- Any important patterns are missing

**Sheet 4 — Summary:** After reviewing all samples, fill in average scores per category and give a final verdict (Approve / Approve with revisions / Major revisions needed / Reject).

---

### How the Synthetic Data Was Generated

A Python script generates each review by:
1. Randomly selecting a category (weighted: bugs 30%, features 18%, usability 17%, performance 14%, praise 12%, compatibility 10%)
2. Picking a random sentence template for that category
3. Filling in placeholders with random values from word lists (device names, OS versions, features, symptoms, etc.)
4. Assigning a realistic star rating (bugs get 1-2 stars, praise gets 4-5 stars)

**Example template:** "App crashes when I {action}. This started after {version}."
**Example output:** "App crashes when I login. This started after v3.2."

### What We Need From You

1. Fill in the yellow cells in all sheets
2. Be honest. If reviews seem artificial, say so
3. Suggest improvements : missing templates, unrealistic word choices, better distributions
4. Provide your final verdict on Sheet 4
