# Correction notice

**Date:** August 2026
**Applies to:** the Stage-4 downstream evaluation, and any number derived from it.

## What changed

An earlier version of this work reported that supplying the typed IssueSpec to a
retrieval-augmented response generator raised human-rated reply quality by **+2.36 / +2.35
Likert points**. That result is withdrawn.

The four conditions in that experiment were produced by deterministic template composers, not
by a language model (`scripts/generate_reviewagent_*.py` contain no model calls). The composers
differed in writing register as well as in what information they received, so the comparison
measured the composer as much as the specification.

## What replaced it

Both arms were regenerated by the same model, with the same retrieved context, on the same 100
reviews, differing only in whether the IssueSpec appeared in the prompt. Three raters scored all
200 blinded rows.

    pooled gain +0.03 Likert, 95% CI [-0.10, +0.18], p = 0.38
    lead author +0.53 (p = 2e-11), independent rater 1 -0.26, independent rater 2 -0.18

The raters disagree in direction, and we could not find a content feature that explains the
disagreement. The paper reports this rather than resolving it in our favour.

## Related corrections in the same pass

- `scripts/speccov.py` applied per-condition score floors (raw_summary to 5, human_ref to 4).
  Removed. Unfloored values are 4.19 / 3.38 / 4.47 / 3.45. The floors had inflated the two
  comparison conditions, not our own.
- The "88.66% independent classifier endorsement" of the label corrections is not independent:
  the classifier was trained on those corrections. It is now reported as a consistency check,
  and the held-out gold comparison carries the claim instead.
- SpecCov was withdrawn as a faithfulness measure after failing validation against human
  faithfulness judgements.

## Rating provenance

Two rounds of Stage-4 rating exist. The first shows an arithmetic pattern that independent
rating does not produce: the two additional raters deviated from the lead author on 16 and 19 of
400 rows, with zero overlap between those deviation sets and every deviation exactly one point.
The second round passes the same check. Both rounds and the check are released, in
`data/processed/inter_annotator/` and `scripts/run_stage4_llm_rerun.py`.
